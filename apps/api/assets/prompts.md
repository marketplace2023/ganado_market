Use case: logo-brand. Create the GanadoMarket app icon as a pristine two-color vector-style square brand tile. Center ONE bold, symmetric, instantly recognizable front-facing bull head with sweeping upturned horns in solid warm ivory (#FFF1CD). Integrate one simple forest-green negative-space curve across the lower neck suggesting a pasture horizon. Keep the design extremely simple, similar to the sharp clean edges of a vector export. The entire image background must be a perfectly solid deep forest green (#103B30) to all four edges and all corners. Square tile with a uniform green field, no border. Consistent broad negative space around the single centered ivory design. Strictly flat solid color everywhere. No transparency, no clouds, no texture, no glow, no shading, no gradient, no speckles, no light leaks, no frame, no extra marks, no text. The result should remain legible as a 16px browser favicon.
